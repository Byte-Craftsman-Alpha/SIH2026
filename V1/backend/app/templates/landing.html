The Problem Statement · SIH26047

India diagnoses in 2 minutes — because the history never arrives written.

Explained simply: when you visit a government hospital, the doctor gets only a few minutes with you. Your whole medical story — old prescriptions, allergies, past illnesses — stays locked in a plastic bag of paper and in your memory. MediKiosk fixes exactly this “first-mile” gap, in the patient's own language.

⏱️
≈2 min

The 2-to-5-minute consultation

Government OPDs see 4,000–10,000 patients/day (AIIMS Delhi alone ~8,000–10,000). A 67-country systematic review placed India's average primary-care consultation at ~2 minutes — among the shortest on Earth. Inside it, the doctor must elicit history, examine, read old papers, diagnose, counsel and prescribe.

Source: Irving/Hobbs et al., BMJ Open 2017 — 179 studies, 28.5 M consultations · doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2017-017902
📜
98.6%

The plastic bag of paper

98.6% of Indian patients still maintain paper records; 27% have lost them. Prescriptions, lab reports and discharge summaries arrive handwritten, multilingual, chronologically disordered — the physician burns scarce minutes just reading the bag.

Source: Gandhi & Soundappan, Indian J Med Res 2024 · ICMR-RUM network study (4,838 OPD patients), IJMR Feb 2024
🌿
10-point

The AYUSH multiplier

Ayurvedic case-taking requires Trividha, Ashtavidha & Dashavidha Pariksha — Prakriti, Vikriti, Agni, Koshtha, Sara, Samhanana and more. This interview is far deeper than allopathic intake — effectively impossible inside a 2-minute slot, so the assessment that defines personalized AYUSH care gets abbreviated.

Basis: Charaka Samhita, Vimana Sthana · CCRAS/AIIA Prakriti proforma
🛤️
90+ Cr ⟷ 1.6 L

ABDM has rails, but no first mile

90+ crore ABHA health IDs exist (PIB, May 2026) — yet under 1.6 lakh facilities run ABDM software (PIB, Feb 2025), and nothing captures the clinical story into the ecosystem before the encounter. Registration systems capture demographics; the structured history — the payload that makes ABDM valuable — is never created.

Source: PIB 30 May 2026 (90 Cr ABHA) · PIB 11 Feb 2025 (facility count)
🚨
3 silent failures

What the status quo costs — in harm

1 · Missed allergies & interactions: Indian hospitals show a 34% median medication-error incidence, concentrated at prescribing (2025 systematic review of 40 studies). 2 · Missed red flags: chest pain + breathlessness waits 2 hours in a routine queue. 3 · The “blank patient”: 68,584 hit-and-run accidents in 2024 (MoRTH) — unconscious victims treated with zero history.

Sources: Govil et al. 2025 (PMC) · WHO Patient Safety Fact Sheet, 13 Sep 2023 · BMJ Quality & Safety, 2 Oct 2024 (history-taking failures → 2.5× error risk) · MoRTH 2024

📋 What the Problem Statement officially asks for

SIH26047 “Patient Case-Taking Software” (Ministry of Ayush — All India Institute of Ayurveda, Software category, MedTech/BioTech/HealthTech theme) asks for one patient-facing platform where the patient — independently, before entering the consultation room — can:

✓Record their full clinical history by natural spoken conversation + guided touchscreen (multilingual, multi-accent, noisy-hospital robust, zero-training for low-literacy & elderly users)
✓Digitize physical medical documents — handwritten + printed, multilingual OCR with extraction of diagnoses, medications, lab values and procedures
✓Produce a structured, physician-ready summary (Chief Complaint → HPI → Past History → Drugs/Allergies → Family → Personal → ROS → Investigations) that the doctor verifies in seconds
✓Push the record to HIS/EMR and the ABHA-linked PHR via FHIR, under DPDP Act 2023 + ABDM consent governance — with emergency red-flag detection routed to triage

The Solution

One kiosk. Five steps. A finished history before the token is called.

MediKiosk is a patient-facing AI clinical-intake platform with two coordinated windows — the Patient Window (kiosk / companion app: identify → consent → converse → scan → verify) and the Physician Window (portal: AI-drafted summary, source labels, confidence scores, Accept / Amend / Reject). The patient owns the data and the consent; the doctor gets the clinical value.

🪪1

IDENTIFY

≈ 30 s

Language select (हिंदी/English) → ABHA scan / number / phone OTP. New patients: 5-step registration wizard with DPDP consent.

🗣️2

CONVERSE

4–6 min new · 60–90 s returning

Adaptive AI interview — voice OR touch on every question. SOCRATES/OPQRST branching, coverage meter, AYUSH Dashavidha mode, continuous red-flag watch.

📄3

SCAN

2–3 min

Photograph old Rx / lab reports / discharge summaries → quality check → classify → OCR (print + handwriting) → entity extraction with confidence scores.

🧾4

SUMMARIZE & ROUTE

≈ 20 s

Conversational history + verified document data fuse into the standard 12-section physician summary + chronological timeline → pushed to doctor portal → FHIR R4 bundle.

🩺5

CONSULT

≈ 30 s review

Doctor reads a completed, source-labelled history in seconds — amends, verifies, saves. The consultation time goes back to examination & counselling.

A

Conversational Multimodal History Engine

Dual-mode (voice ⊕ touch) adaptive interview constrained by a clinician-approved ontology — chief complaint → SOCRATES/OPQRST follow-ups grounded in the patient's own medication history → completeness meter → AYUSH Mode with all 10 Dashavidha parameters + Agni/Koshtha/Ahara-Vihara, grounded on the CCRAS Prakriti baseline. Deterministic red-flag engine can interrupt the flow in seconds.

B

Document Digitization & Intelligence

6-stage pipeline: Capture → Quality Check → Classify (Rx / Lab / Discharge) → OCR (printed + handwritten, multilingual) → Extract (diagnoses, meds + dose, lab values + reference ranges, procedures) → Chronological timeline. Abnormal values and drug–herb interactions flagged; every field carries a confidence score and a patient-verification gate.

C

Structured Summary + Physician Portal

Standard clinical format — CC → HPI → Past Med/Surg → Drug & Allergy → Family → Personal → ROS → Prior Investigations. Editable draft with Accept / Amend / Reject, source labels (patient-reported · OCR-extracted · patient-verified · physician-verified) and bilingual EN/हिं output. Never an autonomous diagnosis.

D

Consent, Privacy & ABDM Integration

Granular, revocable, audio-explained consent per DPDP Act 2023 (verified live: revocation mutates the DB instantly). ABHA + OTP authentication, 4-hour consent windows on bookings, FHIR R4 bundle push (HAPI sandbox), hash-chained audit logs, session data wipes, and break-glass emergency access with full governance.

✅ What MediKiosk IS

  • ✓A patient-facing AI clinical-intake platform — walk-in kiosk (primary) + optional BYOD companion app
  • ✓A document digitization & intelligence pipeline (OCR → extraction → timeline)
  • ✓A physician verification dashboard — AI drafts, humans decide
  • ✓A consent-first ABDM-compatible layer (ABHA · FHIR · HIS)
  • ✓An emergency break-glass service + tamper-evident, hash-chained record layer

⛔ What MediKiosk is NOT

  • ✕Not a diagnostic engine — it never diagnoses, never prescribes, never replaces physician judgment
  • ✕Not a blockchain gimmick — lightweight hash-chained audit anchoring keeps records tamper-evident and DPDP-erasable (no medical data on-chain)
  • ✕Not a mobile-app-first design — kiosk is the primary channel per PS §2.2; the app is an optional companion (attendant mode, follow-ups, BYOD)
  • ✕Not a rip-and-replace — it plugs into existing HIS/registration flows and feeds verified summaries into them

Innovation · Novelty · USP

Ten defensible innovations — integration-first, not gimmick-first.

Each claim below is demonstrable in the current build or in the announced roadmap — no slideware.

01

Returning-patient “delta intake” — 60–90 s instead of 6 min

Static profile (Prakriti, allergies, chronic meds) is captured once; each revisit only asks what changed (Vikriti delta). No other intake system does this — it removes repeated questioning across visits, the #1 patient complaint in OPDs.

02

Dual-mode voice ⊕ touch on every question

Most “voice AI” demos fail the literacy test; most touch apps fail the elderly test. MediKiosk treats both as first-class equals: speak “बुखार है” or tap the fever icon — icon-first 48 px targets, TTS prompts, attendant button, mid-flow language switch.

03

Ontology-constrained adaptive dialogue

LLM fluency caged inside clinician-approved question paths (complaint-indexed ontology graph, SOCRATES/OPQRST templates). Adaptive like a doctor, safe like a protocol. Two-call AI pattern: structured proposal → independent safety verifier → grounded response, with deterministic ontology-graph fallback on rejection/quota.

04

Fused intake: conversation + paper → one verified timeline

Nobody else merges what the patient says with what the patient carries at point of entry. OCR-extracted meds/labs fuse with interview answers into a single chronological, source-labelled, confidence-scored record.

05

Deterministic red-flag safety layer (non-LLM)

Emergency escalation never depends on probabilistic AI. Clinician-tuned rule patterns (e.g. chest pain + sweating + breathlessness) fire a non-dismissible full-screen alert, an 8-second triage/family dispatch countdown, Call-108 and GPS-SMS actions — verified on real device (TC-CHAT).

06

AYUSH-native by design — the sponsor's own clinical language

Full Dashavidha Pariksha (10/10 parameters) as a configurable mode, CCRAS-standard 18-question Prakriti assessment, Trividha split (Prashna→AI voice · Darshana→camera-assisted · Sparshana→physician), Ashtavidha kept doctor-side, and a NAMASTE → ICD-11 Ch.26 → FHIR R4 mapping path — national-scale AYUSH interoperability no allopathic system attempts.

07

Hash-chained provenance: source → field → summary line

Every extracted field carries provenance + confidence; finalized records are SHA-256 hash-anchored into an append-only, tamper-evident audit chain — integrity without blockchain's latency/cost, and fully compatible with DPDP erasure rights (no medical data on-chain).

08

Break-glass emergency access via ABHA

The “blank patient” solution: verified HFR-registered facility staff can retrieve a minimal emergency view (blood group, allergies, anticoagulants, implants, comorbidities) of an unresponsive patient in ≤2 minutes — 100% audit-logged, post-hoc privacy-officer reviewed, patient-notified. Turns ABDM from a filing system into a life-saving tool.

09

Modern drug–herb interaction flagger

Cross-system safety for AYUSH reality: flags simultaneous Triphala Churna + Metformin (hypoglycaemia risk) during OCR extraction — demonstrated live in the test report. Scope-honest: a flagging engine for physicians, not a prescription authority.

10

Dual-environment deployment (cloud ⇄ offline kiosk)

One codebase, two profiles: Production (Vercel serverless + Supabase cloud) and Beta (local FastAPI + SQLite on hospital LAN) — switchable in-app. This is the offline/low-connectivity strategy for PHCs: store-and-forward sync, UPS-friendly, manual-intake fallback always available.

Feasibility & Execution Viability

Verdict: HIGH — for the MVP scope we honestly state.

High for printed OCR + assisted handwriting with human gates, hi/en voice with touch fallback, sandbox FHIR/ABDM — every component is mature or government-backed. The innovation is in integration and workflow design, exactly what a hackathon-grade team can ship and what the 8-week hardening plan scales.

🔧

Technical feasibility

  • ASR/TTS for Indian languages: mature via Bhashini & AI4Bharat (GoI NLTM); noisy-OPD robustness is engineering (mic placement, touch fallback), not research
  • OCR: production-grade engines exist; handwriting is the hardest nut → mitigated by confidence labels + patient-verify + physician gate (never silently trusted)
  • Constrained clinical dialogue: ontology + templates + verifier = demonstrable today (it is demonstrated — see live demo)
  • ABDM/FHIR: public sandboxes, documented APIs, mock-first strategy de-risks the demo
🏥

Operational feasibility

  • Kiosk = commodity tablet + mic/speaker/camera; pilot runs on 2–4 tablets
  • Staff role becomes supervisory (assist + triage ack) — net reduction in HR load, not addition
  • Phased migration: standalone pilot → registration adapter → HIS/EMR push → FHIR/ABDM linking. No big-bang replacement
  • Graceful degradation: manual intake always remains possible; decline-consent → today's flow unchanged
⚖️

Regulatory feasibility

  • DPDP 2023 compliant by design: purpose limitation, minimization, granular revocable consent, correction & erasure rights (no on-chain data)
  • ABDM consent-manager artefacts; ABHA-linked sharing only with explicit consent
  • Clinical governance: system never diagnoses/prescribes; physician verification mandatory before HIS write; red-flag rules clinician-approved & versioned
  • Data localization: hospital private cloud / MeghRaj deployable
📏

How we measure success (eval metrics)

  • ASR word-error rate target: WER ≤ 15% (Hindi, hospital noise)
  • OCR field-F1 targets: ≥ 0.85 printed · ≥ 0.70 handwriting-assisted
  • Physician edit-distance on summaries — trending down across pilot weeks
  • 50-case clinician pilot (Wk 7–8) with gold-set reporting; red-flag sensitivity/specificity + false-flag review loop published
  • Prakriti questionnaire: CCRAS/AIIA clinician validation + inter-rater check scheduled before pilot sign-off

Impact & Benefits

Who gains what — patient, doctor, hospital, Ministry.

Impact logic: high patient volume → short consultations → history drives most diagnoses → paper friction → ABDM opportunity → MediKiosk. Figures below are pilot targets (goals, not achieved results) — stated honestly, measured in the 50-case pilot.

🧑‍🦱

Patient

Speaks in their own language; tells the story once, not at every visit; allergies & interactions surfaced before prescribing; paper records never “lost” again; personalized (Prakriti-aware) AYUSH care; emergency history accessible even when unconscious.

👩‍⚕️

Doctor

A verified, structured, source-labelled history on screen before the token is called — 30-second review replaces ~8 minutes of questioning; red flags pre-routed; medico-legal exposure reduced by documented history.

🏥

Hospital

Higher OPD throughput; fewer duplicated investigations (prior reports findable); audit-ready records; analytics on case-mix; staff redeployed from transcription to supervision.

🏛️

Ministry of Ayush / Public health

Structured AYUSH intake at population scale; ABDM rails finally carrying clinical payloads; NAMASTE→ICD-11 interoperable data; population-level insight from OPDs.
